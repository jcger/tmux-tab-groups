# Privacy Policy for Tmux Tab Groups

**Last Updated: 8 October 2025**

## Data Collection and Storage

Tmux Tab Groups does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

### What We Access:
- **Tab Information**: The extension accesses your tab URLs and titles to enable navigation and hibernation features
- **Bookmarks**: When you hibernate a tab group, URLs and titles are saved as bookmarks in your browser

### What We Store:
- **Locally Only**: All data is stored locally on your device in Chrome's bookmark system
- **No External Servers**: No information is ever sent to external servers or third parties
- **No Tracking**: We do not use analytics, cookies, or any tracking mechanisms

### Your Data:
- Remains entirely on your device
- Is under your control through Chrome's bookmark manager
- Can be deleted at any time by removing the "Tmux Tab Groups" bookmark folder
